Who we are

Friends of the Poor

Philoptochos (fee-LOHP-toh-hos) means “friend of the poor.” St. Barbara Philoptochos is our parish’s chapter of the National Philoptochos Society, serving the poor, the sick, the grieving and the forgotten — in our parish, across the Valley and around the world.

  • Charitable, benevolent and philanthropic outreach
  • Care for the poor, the hungry, the aged, the sick and the distressed
  • Preserving and passing on the Orthodox faith and traditions

“Inasmuch as you did it to one of the least of these My brethren, you did it to Me.” (Matthew 25:40)
